- Instrument: huge dense black wordmark on a clean white field. Small useful navigation, content-led image/video region. At intermediate state, work dominates rather than icon cards. Adapt: strong typographic scale and visual restraint. Source https://www.instrument.com/
- Work & Co: white field, black direct proposition, a vivid red region/logo and thin red rule. Fine dividers and content hierarchy carry the page. Intermediate page keeps aligned editorial columns. Adapt: a deliberate warm accent, matter-of-fact language, useful information rhythm. Source https://work.co/
- Ramotion: official page content verified through web source, emphasizing digital product/brand work and concrete services. Headless browser encountered a security challenge, so no claim is made about its rendered palette. Adapt only the specific offer and capability-led narrative. Source https://www.ramotion.com/
- Clay and Metalab: official pages reviewed for the offer, discipline labels and product emphasis, not for color claims. https://clay.global/ https://www.metalab.com/

Structural letterform and conceptual system schematic tie the design to the company name and actual software offer. Remove earlier stock-style dashboard painting, repeated cards, serif inserts, marquee, decorative counters and generic tech gradients.
